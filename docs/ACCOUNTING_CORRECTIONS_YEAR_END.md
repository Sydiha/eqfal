# Accounting corrections and year-end continuity (migration 080)

Baseline: main `195f9bf`. Nothing here is applied to any database; migration 080 is additive.

## 1. Posted journal reversal (implemented)
- `POST /api/journals/:id/reverse` body `{accounting_date, reason}`; capability `accounting.journal.reverse` (critical; granted to no default role; Full Access / System Admin hold it through the wildcard).
- Creates a NEW posted journal with debit/credit swapped, same account lines, linked by `reversal_of_journal_id` (+ `reversal_reason`). The original is only locked and read; posted-journal triggers still forbid any change to it.
- Eligible: posted, `standard`, no `source_type` (manual), not itself a reversal, not already reversed. Refused with stable codes: `JOURNAL_REVERSAL_NOT_POSTED`, `_SYSTEM_GENERATED`, `_NOT_ELIGIBLE` (opening balance), `JOURNAL_IS_REVERSAL`, `JOURNAL_ALREADY_REVERSED`, `_VAT_TAGGED`, `_HAS_DEPENDENCY`.
- The reversal goes through the canonical posting contract, so the fiscal-year guard, monthly-period guard, balance check and inactive-account check apply to the reversal date. Date must be on/after the original and not in the future; its fiscal year is resolved from the date.
- Duplicates/concurrency: row lock on the original + partial unique index (one reversal per journal) + DB trigger backstop.
- Audit: `journal.reverse` with reason, reversal id, date. Read models expose `reversed_by_journal_id`.
- UI: "Reverse journal" on posted manual journals (confirmation dialog, date + reason, AR/EN).

## 2. Approved invoice corrections (verified, no code change)
F4 already covers the safe path: an approved+posted invoice is corrected by registering an external credit/debit note (separate draft journal, VAT verified against the approved note, same obligation adjusted). Draft/submitted invoices can be cancelled. Nothing was added because the remaining cases need tax/accounting decisions. Journals generated by invoices/notes are deliberately NOT reversible by item 1.

### Deferred (owner decision needed, not guessed)
1. Cancelling an approved, not-yet-posted invoice (obligation guards, draft journal, immutable VAT snapshot, effect on VAT reports).
2. Cancelling a posted invoice or an approved credit/debit note (needs a source-specific reversal incl. VAT return effect).
3. Credit/debit note numbering series; notes outside the original VAT period (amended VAT return); notes on asset invoices.
4. Reversal of system-generated journals (depreciation, disposal, accrual schedule lines, settlements, bank import) and of VAT-tagged journals: each needs a source-specific workflow.
5. Retained-earnings account policy (see 3): which account, and whether a closing journal is wanted.

## 3. Fiscal-year closing and opening continuity
Trace (verified in code): closing a year only flips status after readiness checks. Trial balance and every statement are computed per `fiscal_year_id` from posted journals of that year; a year's brought-forward position is its single `opening_balance` journal, created from a manually entered review. `/suggestions` only proposes receivables/payables/assets/custody/bank evidence, never the prior year's ledger, and nothing computed retained earnings. So carry-forward between consecutive years was entirely manual and could omit or double-count balances.

Implemented model (no closing journal; compatible with the per-year reports): `GET|POST /api/fiscal-years/:id/carryforward`.
- Source: final trial balance of a CLOSED year (all posted journals incl. its opening journal). Balance-sheet accounts are carried at net balance; revenue/expense accounts net into the retained-earnings account the caller names (must be an active equity account classified `equity`).
- Output: ONE draft `opening_balance_review` for the next consecutive year (starts the day after) with `internal_ledger` items, plus an immutable `fiscal_year_carryforwards` record (totals, account, actor). Nothing is posted; the owner submits/approves through the existing opening-balance flow (`opening_balance.approve`), which posts the opening journal under all guards.
- Safety: refuses if the year is open, next year missing/closed, first month closed, an opening review/journal already exists, a carry-forward already exists, ledger unbalanced, carried account inactive. Idempotent and concurrency safe (year row locks + unique constraints). Prior-year statements are untouched (comparatives preserved); the new year starts with zero P&L and opening equity that already holds the prior result, so nothing is counted twice.
- Capability `fiscal_year.carryforward.prepare` (sensitive, no default role). Preview needs `accounting.view` + `opening_balance.view`.
- Items stay editable while the review is draft; the carry-forward record keeps the prepared totals as the audit baseline.

### Limits / deferred
- Carried items use generic categories (other asset / other liability / equity); sub-ledger detail (counterparty, asset, bank links) is not recreated. Dividends/drawings stay in their equity accounts (no policy applied).
- No year-end closing journal and no reopening of a closed year (separate decisions).
- Zakat/income-tax accrual is not computed.
